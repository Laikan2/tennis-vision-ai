Causes and corrections
1. Floor depth candidates matured before sufficient transverse rebound evidence.
   Once emitted, the candidate consumed the incoming track and could not improve from REVIEW.
   Floor depth-only candidates now wait a bounded 120 ms after the estimated turn, permitting a coherent transverse fit to mature first.
   The preview does not wait for this confirmation.
2. Variable apparent ball size could invalidate the constant-size floor fit.
   A new bounded perspective-normalized fit requires resolved observed positions on both legs, continuity, stable brightness, bounded size change and actual transverse reversal.
   It retains the estimated point/error in original image coordinates.
   Pure approach/retreat, hands, missing frames and alternating size noise do not gain automatic IN/OUT.
3. Unresolved stationary fragments could retain track ownership.
   A bounded cache marks stationary unresolved/elongated fragments; clean still balls and held balls are retained.
   Reacquisition compares multiple moving candidates over two frames and leaves equal alternatives ambiguous.
   An initial regression on 3 px targets was found and corrected; subpixel-size variation keeps the earlier tolerances.
4. Pausing previously retained marks without a clear finish action.
   Encerrar treino saves the completed session before clearing live circles, pulses and counters.
   It stops analysis/audio, starts a fresh session and retains the current area.
   Pausing retains review marks. History remains accessible, including stored evidence and manual review.
   Queued old contacts cannot enter the next training interval. A storage error retains the old session instead of reporting a successful finish.

Limits
- No physical camera test of 0.6.22 has been completed in this turn. The attached phone/archive was inspected read-only.
- Wall returns remain REVIEW because one camera cannot prove that an image-space reversal touched the wall rather than a hand/racket.
- A movement supported only by changing apparent size remains REVIEW. Near-line uncertainty, insufficient pixels, occlusion and camera/area mismatch still prevent automatic IN/OUT.
- The changes do not recover a ball hidden in blur or absent between captured frames; they do not certify a 200 km/h serve or full-court accuracy.
- Camera placement, exposure, light, distance and measured analysis cadence still determine usable evidence.
- Speed is an apparent lateral estimate based on a 67 mm ball; unavailable estimates are not replaced with fabricated values.
